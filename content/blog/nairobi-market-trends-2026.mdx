Nairobi's residential market enters 2026 with a steadiness that few predicted three years ago. After the correction of 2023 and 2024, asking prices in the established suburbs have flattened rather than fallen, and transaction volumes are recovering as mortgage rates ease off their peaks. If you are planning to buy or sell this year, here are the trends worth your attention.

## Apartment supply in Kilimani and Westlands is finally thinning

Between 2017 and 2022, Kilimani and Westlands absorbed wave after wave of new apartment blocks, and rents for two and three bedroom units paid the price. That pipeline has narrowed. Developers who paused projects during the high interest rate period are only now restarting, which means completions in 2026 will run well below the 2019 peak.

For buyers, the practical effect is negotiating leverage on completed but unsold units. For landlords, it means the worst of the rent softening is behind us, particularly for well managed buildings with borehole water and backup power, the two amenities tenants now ask about before anything else.

## The bypass effect on satellite towns is real

The Nairobi Expressway, the Southern Bypass and the ongoing dualling of the Northern Bypass have redrawn the commuting map. Ruaka, Syokimau, Kitengela and Athi River have all seen measurable land price growth within a short radius of their interchanges.

We track land transactions across these corridors, and the pattern is consistent: plots within three kilometres of a bypass access point have appreciated roughly 40 to 60 percent over five years, while comparable plots five kilometres further out moved far less. The lesson for land buyers is simple. Pay for access, not for distance.

## Family buyers are moving up, not out

An interesting shift in the last eighteen months: families who once planned to leave Nairobi for Kiambu or Machakos are instead upsizing within the city's garden suburbs. Karen, Runda, Hardy and Ruaka townhouse and maisonette stock has seen steady absorption, and gated developments of eight to twelve units are the sweet spot.

Buyers tell us the same thing: school runs, weekend traffic and work from home habits have made a forty minute commute feel more expensive than a slightly bigger mortgage closer in.

## What this means for you

If you are buying to live in, the 2026 market rewards decisiveness in the established suburbs because well priced, well located stock still moves within sixty days. If you are investing, follow the infrastructure: bypass adjacent land and small scale rental developments in the satellite towns offer the clearest upside on a five to seven year view.
